# Presentation

Put the PDF of your 5-minute presentation here as `presentation.pdf`. Use any tool you like, but I
strongly suggest you do not use AI-generated slides.

Your slides should cover:

- your datasets and why you find them interesting
- your models, and why each one is underfit, well fit or overfit
- how the feature effect explanations change with the fit of the model
- what that means in reality for someone relying on these explanations
